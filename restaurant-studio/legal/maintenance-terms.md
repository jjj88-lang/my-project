# Monthly Website Maintenance: Terms

> DRAFT written without a lawyer. Have an attorney review it before use. See [README.md](README.md).
> Attached to the [Website Project Agreement](service-agreement.md). Numbers come from `config/pricing.json`.

**Price:** $59 per month. Card processing fees are paid by the Studio.

## What is covered

- [ ] **Hosting** on the Studio's managed account, with the security certificate (https).
- [ ] **Up to 60 minutes of small changes each month:** menu items, prices, hours, photos and announcements.
- [ ] **Software and security updates** to the site.
- [ ] **Monitoring** that the site and its forms are working.
- [ ] **Replies within two business days**, or **one business day if the site is down.**

## How the 60 minutes work

- Send change requests by email to [STUDIO EMAIL]. One message with several changes is easier to handle than many small ones.
- Time is counted in real minutes and logged. You can ask for the log at any time.
- Unused minutes do not carry over to the next month. [OWNER TO CONFIRM]
- If a request will take you past 60 minutes in a month, the Studio tells you first and gives an estimate. Extra time is $60 per hour (minimum charge 30 minutes), and nothing extra is done until you approve it.
- Fixing a problem the Studio caused never uses your minutes.

## What is not covered

- New pages, new sections, a redesign or new features.
- Writing large amounts of new text, or translating.
- Professional photography or logo design.
- The online ordering or reservation system itself, or changes inside it.
- Paid advertising, social media management or ongoing SEO campaigns.
- Business email accounts.
- Your domain name's yearly fee (paid by you, to your registrar, in your name).

These can be quoted separately at $60 per hour.

## Response times

A reply means a real answer and a plan, not always a finished change. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays. The Studio is one person: if they will be unavailable for more than two business days, they tell maintenance clients in advance and say how an outage will be handled in that time. [OWNER TO CONFIRM a backup arrangement]

## Billing

- Billed monthly through a Stripe subscription, starting on the launch date.
- Receipts come by email from Stripe.
- If a payment fails, the Studio emails you. If it is still unpaid 30 days after it was due, the Studio may stop making changes, and after a further written notice of [7] days may take the site offline. Your domain and content stay yours. [OWNER TO CONFIRM these periods]

## Pausing and cancelling

- **Pausing:** the plan cannot be paused while the site stays live, because hosting continues. If the restaurant closes for a season, you can cancel and sign up again later. [OWNER TO CONFIRM]
- **Cancelling:** month to month. Cancel any time with **30 days' notice** by email. You pay for the notice period, and the site stays live and supported until it ends.
- The Studio may also end the plan with 30 days' notice.

## What happens to the site when the plan ends

1. The Studio sends you the complete site files (the code as built for your restaurant and your content), as described in the project agreement's ownership section.
2. The Studio helps you move the site to a hosting account in your name during the notice period. Choose a plan that allows commercial use (Vercel's free Hobby plan does not: https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines, as of November 2026).
3. Once the move is confirmed, or the notice period ends, the Studio removes the site from its hosting account.
4. Your domain stays in your account. You or the Studio point it to the new host.
