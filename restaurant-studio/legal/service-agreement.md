You get **two rounds of revisions**. A round is one consolidated list of changes, sent in one message, that the Studio makes together. Changes beyond two rounds, or new features not in the SOW, are billed at **$60 per hour** (minimum charge 30 minutes), only after you approve an estimate in writing.
