# Weekly rhythm

About 8 to 10 hours a week, around classes. The business works if it is steady, not if it is big. Adjust the days to your timetable; keep the blocks.

## A typical week

| Block | Time | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox, every weekday | 15 minutes | Read and reply. Anything that needs real work goes on the list for a work block. Site down or a wrong price live: fix it today. |
| Build block 1 | 2 hours | The build or revision with the nearest date. |
| Build block 2 | 2 hours | The other build in progress, or launch prep. |
| Outreach | 2 hours | The sales routine in `sales/README.md`. Visits fit best mid-afternoon, between lunch and dinner service. |
| Maintenance | 30 to 60 minutes | Change requests, logged as you go (`09-maintenance.md`). First week of the month: the monthly routine. |
| Weekly review | 20 minutes | The checklist below, at the end of the week. |

That is roughly 8 to 9 hours. Leave the rest as buffer. Weeks always need it.

## Capacity limits

- **No more than two builds in progress.** A build is in progress from deposit to launch. A third client gets an honest start date: "I can start on [date]. Your deposit isn't due until then."
- **No more than one launch a week**, and never launch the day before an exam.
- **Maintenance clients:** each takes up to about 1.5 hours a month (routine plus their 60 minutes). At eight, that is about three hours a week. Before taking a ninth, review whether the week still fits.
- **Do not start a new project in the last two weeks before finals.**

## When exams come

Two weeks before:

- [ ] No new kickoffs. Finish or pause builds at a clean point (a draft sent and waiting for feedback is a good pause).
- [ ] Pause outreach. Do not book discovery calls in exam weeks.
- [ ] Email every active and maintenance client with the dates:

  > A heads-up: I have exams from [date] to [date]. During that time I'll handle anything urgent, like the site being down or a wrong price, within one business day. Other changes I'll do from [date]. If anything changes on your side before then, send it now and I'll get it in.

- [ ] Set an email auto-reply for the exam period that says the same.

During exams: only outages and wrong information on live sites. Nothing else. Who covers an outage if you truly cannot is an open question for the owner (`legal/maintenance-terms.md`, Response times).

## Weekly review (20 minutes)

- [ ] **Money:** any deposit or balance requested more than 7 days ago? Send the reminder (`05-collecting-payment.md`). Any failed maintenance payment in Stripe?
- [ ] **Builds:** for each build, the next step and its date. Anything waiting on the owner for more than 5 business days gets a nudge.
- [ ] **Materials:** anything due this week?
- [ ] **Maintenance:** minutes used per client this month. Anyone close to 60?
- [ ] **Launches:** fix windows ending this week. Send a short "the fix window ends Friday, anything you've noticed?" message.
- [ ] **Outreach:** CRM follow-ups due next week (`sales/README.md`).
- [ ] **Next week:** exams, deadlines, travel. Cut work before you cut sleep.
- [ ] **One improvement:** one thing that took longer than it should have. Write it down; fix the doc or template if it keeps happening.
