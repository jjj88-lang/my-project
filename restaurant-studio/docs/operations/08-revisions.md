# 08. Revisions

Every project includes **two rounds of revisions** (section 4 of the agreement). Work beyond that, or anything not in the statement of work, is **$60 per hour, minimum charge 30 minutes**, and only after the owner approves an estimate in writing.

## What a round is

- One consolidated list of changes, sent in one message, that you make together and send back as one updated preview.
- Fixing your own mistake (a price typed wrong, a broken link, a misspelled dish) **never** counts as a round. Say so when you fix one.
- A small follow-up within a day ("oh, and the Tuesday hours") belongs to the same round. Do not count it against them.

## Collecting one list

Owners send changes in pieces: a text during service, a voice note, a comment from a server. Make it easy to send one list, then turn whatever arrives into one list yourself.

1. Ask for one message (the wording is in `04-review-and-approval.md`).
2. If pieces arrive anyway, collect them for one day, then write them back as a numbered list:

   > Here is everything I have for round one:
   > 1. Spicy tuna roll: $9, not $8.
   > 2. Home page: use the photo of the grill.
   > 3. Add "Closed Thanksgiving Day" to the hours.
   > Is that everything? Once you say yes, I'll make the changes and send the updated version by [date].

3. Start work only after they confirm the list. Log the list and the date in `log.md`.
4. Aim to send each updated preview within 2 business days.

## In scope or not

| Usually part of a round | Out of scope (quote it) |
| --- | --- |
| Text, prices, hours, photos swapped, sections reordered, colors adjusted | A new page or a page type the package does not include |
| Moving a button, changing a headline | Online ordering, reservations or payments built into the site |
| Choosing a different font preset or hero layout | Logo design, photography, writing a long story from scratch |
| Adding menu items the owner forgot | Translation, a blog, an email newsletter |
| | Upgrading Starter to Premium (quote the difference: $999 minus $699 = $300) |

## Saying "that's outside the project" kindly

Be specific, give a price or an alternative, and let them choose.

> That's a good idea. A separate private-events page isn't part of the Starter package, so it would be extra: about 1 hour, $60. If you'd like it, reply "yes" and I'll add it after launch. Or I can add a short private-events line to the home page now as part of this round, at no charge.

> Built-in online ordering isn't something I build, because you already have [system] and it handles payments and kitchen tickets. I'll make the Order button go straight to it.

## A third round

> We've used both revision rounds. I'm happy to keep going: this list looks like about 45 minutes, so $45 at $60 an hour. Reply "yes" and I'll start. Or, if you'd rather launch now, small changes later are covered by the maintenance plan if you choose it.

Bill it with an invoice from `tools/invoice/invoice.html` or a Stripe invoice (`docs/payments.md`), after the work is approved.
