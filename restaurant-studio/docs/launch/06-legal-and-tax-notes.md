# 06. Legal and tax notes

**This is not legal or tax advice.** It is a list of questions to confirm with the people who can
answer them: the state, the IRS, an accountant, an attorney, or your university. None of the items
below was verified. They are leads from the team's notes, and the web addresses were written from
memory and not opened. Check each one on the official page and write the answer and the date next
to it.

## Items that affect client sites (tell the client, do not decide for them)

- **Raw or undercooked food advisory.** Sushi menus usually carry an asterisk and a reminder. The
  FDA Food Code (section 3-603.11) is a model code, adopted locally, and does not mention
  websites. New York outside New York City uses its own Subpart 14-1. The restaurant should ask its
  inspector. Check: https://www.fda.gov/food/fda-food-code/food-code-2022 , the Kansas Department of
  Agriculture, and Tompkins County Whole Health.
- **Website accessibility.** The Department of Justice says the ADA applies to the websites of
  businesses open to the public, and points to WCAG without setting a technical rule for private
  businesses. We build toward WCAG 2.1 AA as a good-faith effort and guarantee nothing. Check:
  https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/

Until an official page or a professional confirms it, treat every "lead" on this page as a question.
