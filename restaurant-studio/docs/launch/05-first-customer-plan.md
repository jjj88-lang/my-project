# 05. First customer plan (14 days)

Goal: one signed agreement and a paid deposit. This plan cannot promise that. What it can do is
make sure about 20 restaurants get a careful look and about 10 get a real conversation, without
eating your semester. Budget: about 10 hours a week.

Start only when `04-launch-checklist.md` sections 1 to 6 are done. If the clock starts mid-November
2026, Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26) falls inside it: no visits or calls that week from
Wednesday to Friday, and expect slow replies.

## Rules that do not bend

- Check each restaurant's site on your own phone before anyone is contacted. Write what you saw.
- One restaurant at a time, one personal message each. No bulk email, no scraped personal emails,
  no texts to cell phones, no automated dialers.
- Real names and notes live only in `private/` (gitignored), never in a tracked file.
- Contact only during the quiet gap, about 2 to 4 pm on weekdays. Never Friday or Saturday nights.
- Never promise more customers or sales. Offer convenience, a clean site and reliable support.

Playbook: `../../sales/README.md`. CRM: `../../tools/crm/README.md`.

Totals: about 20 phone checks, 10 to 12 contacts, at most two follow-ups per restaurant.

## What to say

Lead with one thing you actually saw, then offer to show, not sell. For example: "I opened your
site on my phone and the menu is a PDF I had to zoom into. I build simple restaurant sites where the
menu is real text with prices. Could I show you a sample on my phone? It takes two minutes."
If they say no, thank them and stop. If they say "not now", ask whether you may check back in a
month, and write that date in the CRM.

## What to track (in the CRM, every contact)

Date, route (visit, call, email), who you spoke to (role, not a personal email you found
somewhere), what you saw on their site, their answer, the next step and its date. If someone asks
not to be contacted again, mark it the same day and never contact them again.

## Weekly review (Day 7 and Day 14)

- How many checks, contacts, conversations, calls, proposals?
- Which observation got the best response? Which route?
- Any objection that came up twice? Add an honest answer to your notes (`../../sales/09-objections.md`).

## If nothing lands by Day 14

That is common and not a verdict. Change one thing at a time:

1. No conversations at all: shift from email to visits; try a different time in the 2 to 4 pm window.
2. Conversations but no calls: show the sample sooner and talk less. Ask what bothers them about
   their current site, then listen.
3. Calls but no yes: ask what held them back. Check whether the Starter package fits better.
4. Widen the list beyond the priority cuisines to any small independent restaurant.
5. Ask restaurants you already know (former employers, places you have worked near) whether they
   would look at a sample. Personal connections are fine; pressure is not.

Then run the same 14 days again with the change.
