# Sales playbook

How Sidework Studio finds and signs restaurant clients, one restaurant at a time. Every file here is short enough to read on your phone in the parking lot.

## How it fits together

1. **Research** a restaurant and check its site on your own phone: [01-prospect-research.md](01-prospect-research.md). Record what you saw in the CRM ([../tools/crm/README.md](../tools/crm/README.md)).
2. **Reach out once**, by the route that suits that restaurant: email ([02](02-cold-email.md)), phone ([05](05-phone-script.md)) or a visit ([06](06-in-person.md)). Use one verified observation ([03](03-personalized-pitch.md)).
3. **Follow up twice at most**, then stop: [04-follow-up.md](04-follow-up.md).
4. **Talk for 15 minutes**: [07-discovery-call.md](07-discovery-call.md). Answer concerns honestly: [09-objections.md](09-objections.md).
5. **Send a one-page proposal**: [08-proposal-template.md](08-proposal-template.md).
6. **Ask for the decision** and start: [10-closing.md](10-closing.md), then [11-onboarding-checklist.md](11-onboarding-checklist.md).

Leave-behind for visits: [print/one-pager.html](print/one-pager.html) (open in a browser, print on US Letter).

Merge fields look like `{{restaurant}}`, `{{observation}}`, `{{your_name}}`. Replace every one before you send anything.

## A weekly rhythm for a part-time student

Plan for about four to six hours a week.

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday evening | Research 3 to 5 restaurants. Phone check each one. Update the CRM. | 60 to 90 min |
| Tuesday or Wednesday, 2 to 4 pm | Visit or call 2 or 3 restaurants. This is the quiet gap between lunch and dinner, when an owner might have five minutes. | 60 to 90 min |
| Thursday, 2 to 4 pm | Send personal emails to anyone you could not reach in person. Send follow-ups that are due. | 30 to 45 min |
| Weekend morning | Discovery calls, proposals, and client work. | as needed |

Never visit or call during lunch (about 11 am to 2 pm) or dinner (about 5 to 9 pm), on Friday or Saturday nights, or on a holiday.

## Honest expectations

Most restaurants will not reply. That is normal and says nothing about you. Owners are busy, many already have someone, and many simply do not want to spend money this month. We do not have response-rate numbers yet, so do not set a target from someone else's statistics. Track your own in the CRM after your first 20 contacts and adjust from there.

In-person visits usually lead to better conversations than email, because the owner can see the demo and see you. Email is for when a visit is not practical.

## The rules

1. **Verify first.** Open the restaurant's site on your own phone before you contact anyone. Our research notes come from search results, not from looking at the sites, and search has been wrong. Never mention a problem you have not seen yourself.
2. **One restaurant at a time.** Every message is written for one restaurant. No bulk email, no mail-merge blasts, no automated dialers, no texts to cell phones.
3. **Public business contacts only.** The published business phone, the email the restaurant lists, the front door. No scraped or guessed personal emails.
4. **Stop when asked.** If anyone says no, not interested, or do not contact us, mark them "Do not contact" in the CRM the same day and never contact them again.
5. **No promises about customers or sales.** We offer convenience, a professional presentation and reliable support. See [09-objections.md](09-objections.md).
6. **Be kind about their current site.** Someone they know may have built it. Describe what you saw, never judge it.
7. **Sometimes the right answer is "you do not need us."** Say so. It is good for your reputation.

## Before your first email

CAN-SPAM requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. Fill in `mailingAddress` in `config/studio.json` first. See [02-cold-email.md](02-cold-email.md).
