# 05. Phone script

## When and what number

- Call only the restaurant's **published business number**, dialed by hand by you.
- Only off-peak: about 2 to 4 pm, Monday to Thursday. Never during lunch, dinner, or a Friday or Saturday night.
- No autodialers, no prerecorded or AI voice, no marketing texts. FCC rules require prior written consent for those to cell phones, and a restaurant's number may be a cell phone.
- If anyone says "don't call again", say "Of course, sorry to bother you", and mark "Do not contact" the same day.

(The rules for hand-dialed calls to a business line are summarized from FTC and FCC pages; state rules can be stricter. Treat this as general guidance, not legal advice.)

## Who answers, and what to say

**Whoever picks up (host, server, manager):**

> Hi, my name is {{your_name}}. I'm not a customer and I'm not calling about an order. I build websites for local restaurants. Is the owner or manager around, or is there a better time to reach them?

If they ask what it's about:

> I looked at your website on my phone and had a small idea about the menu. It's a two-minute conversation, no rush.

If the owner is not in: ask for a good day and time, write it in the CRM, and thank them. Do not leave a long message with staff.

## The 30-second opener (owner or manager)

> Hi, thanks for picking up. I'm {{your_name}}, a student here in {{city}}, and I build simple websites for independent restaurants. Is now an okay time for 30 seconds, or should I call back?
>
> *(If yes:)* I looked up your menu on my phone last week and {{observation}}. *(Only say this if you checked it yourself.)* I put together a sample site to show how a menu can read on a phone. Could I come by one afternoon and show you? It takes five minutes.

Then stop talking and listen.

## If it's a bad time

> No problem at all. When's a quieter time to call, or would you rather I just email you a link?

If they sound rushed or annoyed:

> I'll let you go. Thanks for your time, and have a good service.

## Voicemail (under 20 seconds)

> Hi, this is {{your_name}}, calling for the owner of {{restaurant}}. I build websites for local restaurants and had a small idea about how your menu shows up on phones. No need to call back unless you're curious. My number is {{studio_phone}}, again {{studio_phone}}. Thanks.

Leave one voicemail per restaurant, not one per follow-up.

## After the call

CRM: Outreach status "Called", Last contact today, a one-line note of what was said, and the next step with a date.
