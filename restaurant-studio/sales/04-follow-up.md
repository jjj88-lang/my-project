# 04. Follow-up

Two follow-ups at most, then stop.

| Day | What |
|---|---|
| 0 | First contact (email, call or visit) |
| about 4 business days later | Follow-up 1: add something useful |
| about a week after that | Follow-up 2: a short, kind close |
| after that | Stop. Set status to "Lost" with a note. |

Each follow-up should give them something new: a screenshot, a link, an answer to a likely question. Never "just checking in" or "bumping this up". Same CAN-SPAM footer as [02](02-cold-email.md) on every email. Reply in the same thread so they see the context.

If they said no, or asked you to stop, do not follow up at all.

## Follow-up 1 (about day 4)

**Subject:** Re: {{original_subject}}

Hi {{restaurant}} team,

One more thing that might help. Here's what your menu could look like on a phone, using a sample restaurant I built: {{demo_url}}. It's a fictional place, but the layout is the same one I'd use for you, with your own menu and colors.

If an afternoon visit works better than email, tell me a day and I'll come by between lunch and dinner.

{{your_name}}

{{footer}}

### Other useful things to add instead

- "I checked again today and {{observation}} is still the case." (Only if you checked.)
- "You asked about price: Starter is $699, half to start and half at approval."
- "Here's what you'd need to send me: a menu, your hours and a logo. That's it to start."

## Follow-up 2 (about day 11)

**Subject:** Re: {{original_subject}}

Hi {{restaurant}} team,

I'll leave it here so I'm not filling your inbox. If you ever want help with the menu or the website, my email stays the same and I'm happy to stop by.

Thanks, and good luck with the season.

{{your_name}}

{{footer}}

## After the last one

- CRM: Outreach status "Lost", note why if you know, no next follow-up date.
- You can try again in six months or more only if something real changes (they open a second location, start catering, the site goes down). One message, not a new sequence.
