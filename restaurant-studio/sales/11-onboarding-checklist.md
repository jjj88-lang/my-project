## What you provide

- [ ] **Logo.** The original file if you have one (PNG, SVG or PDF). A clear photo of your sign works too.

- [ ] **Hours,** including holiday hours and any days you close early.
- [ ] **Address and the phone number guests should call.**

- [ ] **Ordering and reservation links,** exactly as you use them now (for example the link your ordering company gave you).
- [ ] **Your domain name.** If you have one: which company it's registered with (the registrar) and who can log in. If you don't: we'll register one together in your name and your account. The yearly fee (about $12 to $20) is paid by you to the registrar.
- [ ] **Catering add-on only:** a Google account that belongs to the restaurant, so the catering spreadsheet is yours.
- [ ] **Anything else you want guests to know:** your story, dietary notes, parking, private parties.
- [ ] **One contact person** for questions and approvals, and the best way to reach them.
