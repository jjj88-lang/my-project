# 07. Discovery call (15 minutes)

Goal: understand what they need, and decide together whether Starter, Premium, the catering add-on, maintenance, or nothing is right. Ask, listen, take notes. Talk for less than a third of the call.

Open with: "Thanks for the time. I'll ask a few questions so I only suggest what you'd actually use. If it turns out you don't need anything, I'll say so."

## How guests find and contact you now

1. **How do most new guests find you?** (Google, walk-by, word of mouth, delivery apps.) If Google or phones come up a lot, the basics in Starter matter most.
2. **What do guests call to ask about most?** Hours, menu, "do you take reservations", catering. Repeated questions point to what the site must answer first.
3. **Do you have a website now? Who controls the domain and the login?** If no one knows, plan time to recover the domain. Ownership is part of what we fix.

## Menu

4. **How many menu sections and items, roughly?** A short menu fits on one page (Starter). A large menu with dietary notes reads better on its own page (Premium).
5. **How often do prices or items change?** Often: recommend maintenance ($59/month, up to 60 minutes of changes).
6. **Do you have the menu as text somewhere (a Word file, the POS)?** If only a photo or printed menu, plan time to type it up.

## Ordering and reservations

7. **Do you take online orders or reservations? Through what?** We link to what they already use. We don't build or sell ordering systems.
8. **Are you happy with that system?** If not, that's their decision with that company. Do not promise to fix it.

## Catering and events

9. **Do you cater or host parties? How do those requests come in now?** Phone during service, or lost messages: the Catering Inquiry Automation add-on ($349) fits.
10. **Do you have a Google account that belongs to the restaurant?** Required for the add-on.

## Photos, logo, story

11. **Do you have a logo file and photos you have the right to use?** Few or none: Starter, which is designed to look finished without photos. Plenty: Premium has a gallery.
12. **Is there a story you want people to know?** (Family recipes, how you started.) If yes, Premium has an Our Story page.

## Who decides, and when

13. **Besides you, who else weighs in on this?** If a partner decides, offer to send the one-page proposal to both.
14. **Is there a date that matters?** (An anniversary, a new menu, catering season.) Use it to plan the timeline, never to pressure.
15. **Would you want me to host and update it, or would you rather have the files and handle it yourselves?** Maintenance yes or no.

## Close the call

Summarize in two sentences what you heard and what you'd suggest, including "you may not need this" if that's true. Then: "I'll send a one-page proposal by {{day}}. Does that work?" Use [08-proposal-template.md](08-proposal-template.md).
