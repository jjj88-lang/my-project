# 06. Walking in

A short visit is often the best first contact. The owner sees a real person and a real demo, and can say no easily.

## When to go

- About 2 to 4 pm, Monday to Thursday. The lunch rush is over and dinner prep has not peaked.
- Not when the dining room is busy, there's a line, or staff are clearly slammed.
- Not on holidays or the weekend before one.

## What to bring

- Your phone, charged, with the demo open and loaded before you walk in (the sample concept that best matches their cuisine).
- One printed one-pager ([print/one-pager.html](print/one-pager.html)) with your name and contact details written or printed in.
- Your notes from the phone check, so you can say exactly what you saw.
- Clean, casual clothes. You're a neighbor, not a salesperson.

## The 20-second introduction

To whoever greets you:

> Hi, I'm not here to eat this time. My name is {{your_name}}. I build websites for local restaurants. Is the owner or a manager around for two minutes? If not, I can just leave a card.

To the owner or manager:

> Thanks. I'm a student here in {{city}} and I worked in restaurants before this. I looked you up on my phone last week and {{observation}}. *(Only if you checked it yourself.)* Can I show you a sample of what a menu could look like? It's a made-up restaurant, but it'll take 30 seconds.

Hand them your phone with the demo. Let them scroll. Say less.

## Reading the room

**Good signs:** they scroll the demo, ask what it costs, mention their own site or a nephew who did it, ask "how long does that take".
Answer the question they asked, briefly. If they want more, offer a 15-minute call or come back at a time they pick ([07](07-discovery-call.md)).

**Signs to leave:** short answers, looking past you at the floor, a ticket printer going, "we're fine". Do not push.

## Leaving gracefully

> I'll let you get back to it. Here's a sheet with my details and prices. If you ever want to talk, I'm easy to reach. Thanks for your time.

Leave the one-pager. Do not ask for their personal number. If they offer it, write down only what they offer.

## After the visit

CRM: Outreach status "Visited", Last contact today, who you spoke to (role is fine, names only if they gave them), what they said, and a follow-up date if they agreed to one. If they said no, mark "Lost" or "Do not contact" as appropriate.
