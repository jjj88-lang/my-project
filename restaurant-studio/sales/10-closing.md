# 10. Closing

Closing is asking a clear question and accepting the answer. No deadlines you made up, no "price goes up Friday", no pressure.

## Ask plainly

After you've sent the proposal and answered their questions:

> Would you like to go ahead with {{package_name}}? If yes, I'll send the agreement and the deposit link today. If not, that's completely fine.

If they hesitate, ask what's on their mind once. Then stop. "Not now" is an answer.

## When they say yes

Same day:

1. **Agreement.** Send the service agreement ([../legal/service-agreement.md](../legal/service-agreement.md)) filled in with their package, price and timeline. They sign before any work starts. CRM Clients tab: "Agreement signed" date.
2. **Deposit.** Send the Stripe Payment Link for 50% of the total. See [../docs/payments.md](../docs/payments.md) and [../tools/stripe/README.md](../tools/stripe/README.md). No card fees are added. CRM: Payment status "Deposit requested".
3. **Onboarding checklist.** Send [11-onboarding-checklist.md](11-onboarding-checklist.md) so they can start gathering materials.

Nothing starts until the agreement is signed and the deposit has arrived.

## What happens in the first week

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Agreement signed, deposit paid. You send a thank-you and confirm what you still need. CRM: "Won", "Deposit paid". |
| Day 1 to 2 | Collect materials. Set up or confirm the domain in their name and their account. If catering automation is included, set up the Google Sheet in their Google account. |
| Day 2 to 3 | You type the menu, build the structure, check every link and phone number. |
| Day 5 (Starter) or 7 (Premium), business days after materials | First draft link sent. Revision round 1 begins. |

Tell them the timeline counts from when you have their materials, so they know a late menu means a later launch. Say it once, kindly.

## Before launch

- Revision rounds done (two included).
- They approve the site in writing (a reply email is enough).
- Send the balance link (50%). CRM: "Balance requested".
- Launch after the balance is paid. CRM: "Paid in full", launch date. The 14-day fix window starts.

## When they say no

> Thanks for considering it. If anything changes, you know where to find me.

CRM: "Lost", with the reason if they gave one.
